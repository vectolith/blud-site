Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, used to identify your workspace and to reply to you.
  • The messages you send to BLUD and the replies it sends, stored so the conversation has context.
  • Access tokens for the accounts you connect (GitHub, Google Calendar, Gmail), stored encrypted.
  • Operational logs of each run: what was asked, which tools ran, whether they succeeded.

Why

To answer your messages, to perform the tasks you ask for, and to keep a record you can inspect or delete. Nothing is used to train third-party models.

Who else sees it

  • The messaging provider that delivers iMessage, which necessarily sees message content in transit.
  • The model provider that generates replies, which receives the conversation needed to answer.
  • The providers whose accounts you connect, when a task requires a call to them.

We do not sell data and we do not share it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a Postgres database and encrypted where it is a credential. You can ask for deletion at any time and the workspace, its messages and its stored credentials are removed.

Your controls

  • Disconnect an account at any time; the stored token is revoked.
  • Request export or deletion by emailing ganiyusail101@gmail.com.
  • Stop messages by replying STOP, which ends delivery.
