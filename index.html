BLUD
Private beta

An assistant you text.
It answers with the work done.

BLUD lives in your iMessage thread. You send a message the way you would to a person. It does the task in the accounts you connected and replies with the result.

What it does

Runs the task, not a description of it

Ask for a summary of a repository, a change to a file, a calendar slot, a document. BLUD performs the work and returns what it produced.

Works in your accounts

You connect GitHub, Google Calendar and Gmail yourself, through the provider's own sign-in. BLUD uses only the accounts you connected.

Keeps your secrets out of the chat

Credentials live in an encrypted vault and are never printed back into the conversation.

One thread, one workspace

Your phone number is your account. Nobody else can reach your data by texting the same number.

How to join

BLUD is in private beta and onboarding is by invitation while the iMessage line is provisioned. Send an email to ganiyusail101@gmail.com with the phone number you want to use.

What BLUD is not

  • It is not a general chatbot shop. There is one assistant, and it is reachable only over iMessage.
  • It does not message anyone on your behalf without you asking for that specific message.
  • It does not run advertising or sell data. There is no analytics script on this page.
